About Us

Nyenge & Company in pursuit of excellence in auditing and accounting is a medium sized firm of Certified Public Accountants (K) and Certified Public secretaries (K) established in 1982 and operates within the country providing dedicated audit and accounting services.

Nyenge and Company believes that provision of effective and efficient financial services to clients forms a basis for institutional and organization growth. This is because having clear financial management records helps institutions and organizations to have a realistic assessment of their operations leading to effective policy and decision making.

What We Do

What We Do

We are inspiring and empowering our people to change the world. Join our community of solvers bringing the skills, technology and ethical decision-making to deliver quality and sustained outcomes that companies, markets and society can count on. Our purpose—to build trust in society and solve important problems—is at the core of everything we do. We provide professional services across two segments: Trust Solutions and Consulting Solutions. Within these segments we bring a range of capabilities to help organizations solve faster, solve more and realize more value. Trust Solutions brings together our Assurance and Tax Reporting capabilities, and Consulting Solutions includes a diverse range of solutions including Tax Consulting. Broadly, Nyenge and Company provides the following services to clients.

Auditing services

At Nyenge & Company, we ensure thorough and independent auditing services. Our team of professionals is committed to delivering accurate insights, safeguarding your business interests, and ensuring compliance with statutory requirements. We take a personalized approach to every audit, ensuring your specific needs are met with precision and efficiency.

Accounting services

We provide comprehensive accounting services, tailored to meet the demands of your business. From bookkeeping to financial reporting, our experts ensure your accounts are in order, compliant with regulations, and delivered in a timely manner. With our services, you can focus on growth, while we handle the numbers.

V.A.T., P. A.Y. E, N.S.S.F. N.H.I. F. advice and computation

We offer expert guidance and computations for V.A.T., P.A.Y.E., N.S.S.F., and N.H.I.F. Our goal is to simplify the complexities of tax and statutory deductions, ensuring that your obligations are met accurately and on time. Let us handle the administrative burden, so you can focus on what matters most—running your business.

Financial management and consultancy services

Our financial management and consultancy services provide organizations with strategic advice and solutions. Whether it’s optimizing cash flow or providing insight into long-term financial planning, our team is equipped to help you make informed decisions that promote financial stability and growth.

Tailor made programmes for organizations and institutions.

We specialize in developing tailor-made programs for businesses, non-profits, and institutions. Our customized solutions are designed to meet your specific operational challenges, ensuring that your organization achieves its objectives efficiently and sustainably. We collaborate closely with clients to design and implement programs that drive success.

Tax advice and consultancy

Nyenge & Company offers specialized tax advice and consultancy to help you navigate the complex tax landscape. We ensure compliance, identify tax-saving opportunities, and provide strategic guidance to minimize your liabilities. Our goal is to optimize your tax position while maintaining compliance with local regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Our Frequently Asked Questions section provides clear answers to common inquiries about NCCPA’s services, including how we support digital transformation, ensure quality, and assist with international tax planning. Explore our FAQs to understand how we can address your business challenges and contribute to your success.

What types of services does NCCPA offer?

NCCPA provides a wide range of services including assurance and audit, consulting, tax, and deals (advisory) services. This includes financial statement audits, strategic consulting, tax planning, risk management, and advisory on mergers and acquisitions.

How can NCCPA help my business with digital transformation?

NCCPA supports digital transformation by integrating advanced technologies such as AI, blockchain, and cloud solutions into your business processes. We provide strategic guidance to enhance operational efficiency, improve data-driven decision-making, and drive innovation.

What is NCCPA’s approach to sustainability?

NCCPA is committed to sustainability through the implementation of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) strategies. We help businesses adopt sustainable practices, comply with regulations, and achieve their long-term sustainability goals while positively impacting the environment and society.

How does NCCPA ensure the quality and reliability of its services?

NCCPA ensures quality and reliability through rigorous standards and methodologies. Our services are guided by global accounting and auditing standards, and we conduct thorough reviews and quality checks to maintain high standards of accuracy, transparency, and integrity.

Can NCCPA assist with international tax planning and compliance?

Yes, NCCPA provides comprehensive global tax services, including international tax planning and compliance. We help businesses navigate complex tax regulations, manage cross-border tax issues, and optimize tax strategies to minimize liabilities and ensure compliance with local and international laws.
